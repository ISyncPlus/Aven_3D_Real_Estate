# Aven — a building you can explore

The project began empty; real estate reference.mp4 was subsequently supplied and reviewed frame by frame. No 3D assets are supplied.
Inventory: Aria, eight storeys, warm beige concrete, deep balconies, charcoal window mullions,
blue-gray dusk, amber interior light, generous glazing, rooftop garden, a planted urban street.
Idea: Aven's eight inhabited levels become an interactive architectural index.
Audience: prospective residents. Primary action: explore a home, then prepare a viewing request.
Argument: experience the building → inspect a floor and a unit → prepare a viewing request.
Copy: “Find your / home / at Aven”. “Thoughtfully designed residences. A little closer to everything.”
Actions: Explore residences. Arrange a visit. Sections: The architecture, Residences, The setting.
Direction: dark-room. The lit building is the exhibit; UI steps aside. Retain the brief's palette.
Explicit brief overrides: three-line heading, full-viewport 3D instead of photographs, discreet bottom controls.
Scope: Next App Router page, CSS, a procedural R3F scene, shared unit data, panels/dialogs,
GSAP camera choreography, local form validation, reduced motion, responsive styles, focused tests.
Architecture: instanced facade/slabs/frames/rails; individual raycast floor targets; procedural trees;
GSAP changes a camera rig, frame loop adds restrained pointer parallax; DOM provides keyboard selection.
No existing callers, fixtures, data, or exports to preserve. No external form service supplied.
All apartment inventory and site location context is illustrative. No actual booking is submitted.

Reference observations: 14.27-second vertical recording of a desktop site; main site sequence occupies about the first 9 seconds. Wide beige gridded facade, darker left return, eight levels over a glazed lobby; building right, headline left; pale dusk sky and dark UI, landscaping/road foreground, amber actions, compact apartment hover panel; controlled orbit transitions into a light-background furnished apartment cutaway and full property panel. Day/night toggle at bottom right. Recreate the architecture and interaction sequence, not the filmed laptop or TikTok marks.

Naming: user requested a different name; finished brand is Aven. The video retains its original Aria branding.
Neighboring architecture: eight individually composed blocks in limestone, brick, and pale plaster; recessed glazing, sills, lintels, mullions, projecting balconies, cornices, roof parapets, services, louvers and solar panels. All repeated detail is instanced.
